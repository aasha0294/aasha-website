Destination weddings

Destination weddings, beautifully brought to life.

A wedding deserves more than a venue. It deserves a place worth travelling to, days that feel like nothing else your family has done together, and a memory people are still describing twenty years from now.

We plan bespoke destination weddings in Udaipur, across India and overseas — the location, the hospitality, the décor, the dining, the entertainment and the running of the days themselves. One team holds all of it, so nothing on the morning of the wedding depends on somebody else remembering.

Why this is different

Most wedding planners hand the travel to an agency. We are the agency.

A Udaipur wedding is a travel problem wearing a wedding dress. Two hundred guests, a dozen airports, elderly relatives who need help changing planes, cousins flying in from Dubai and Toronto who need visas. Planners subcontract that and lose control of it.

We are an IATA accredited travel agency that plans weddings. The tickets are issued by us. When a flight is cancelled at 2am four days before the sangeet, it is our problem and we are already fixing it.

What that means on the day

The guest list and the function sheet are the same document, held by the same people.

  • No planner blaming the travel agent, and no agent blaming the planner
  • One arrival schedule, so cars are at the airport when flights actually land
  • Room blocks matched to who is arriving when, not guessed months ahead
  • Overseas family's visas tracked against the wedding date, not their own

What we take on

The whole wedding, not a slice of it.

You can hand us all of it, or the parts you want handled. Most families start with the venue and the guests, then give us the rest once they see how much of their week it gives back.

Venue

Palace hotels, lakeside lawns, heritage havelis, resorts and farm properties. We shortlist against your guest count and budget, take you to see them, and negotiate the contract.

Design and decor

Mandap, stage, entrance, florals and lighting, worked out function by function and executed on site by teams we have used before.

Catering

Menus tasted and agreed before anything is signed. Counts adjusted as the guest list moves, which it will, right up to the week itself.

Every function

Mehndi, haldi, sangeet, the pheras, the reception. Each one on a timeline, with someone from our office standing there while it happens.

Vendors

Photography and video, makeup, choreography, music, the pandit, sound and lights — booked, briefed, and coordinated so they are not all calling you.

Guest travel and stay

Flights and trains for the whole list, visas for family flying in, hotel room blocks, airport pickups, and cars between venues. This is the part we do better than anyone.

How it works

Six steps, and you always know which one you are on.

Families usually come to us between six and eighteen months out. Less than that is possible — we have done it — but the good venues in season go early.

1

The first conversation

Dates, rough guest count, where people are coming from, and the budget you have in mind. No charge, and no obligation to go further.

2

Venue shortlist and visit

Three or four properties that genuinely fit, with what each would cost. We take you to see them, or send video walkthroughs if you are not in India.

3

A written costing

Every function, every head count, every line — venue, food, decor, vendors, rooms and travel. One document, so you can see where the money is actually going.

4

Vendors and design locked

Decor, catering, photography, music and the rest confirmed and contracted, with the look agreed function by function.

5

Guests moved and housed

The guest list becomes an arrival schedule. Tickets issued, visas filed, rooms blocked, cars assigned to flights.

6

The week itself

Our people are on site from the first arrival to the last departure. You attend your own wedding instead of running it.

The part nobody else wants

Two hundred guests, one arrival schedule.

Tickets issued by us
