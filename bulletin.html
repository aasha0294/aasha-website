Know your destination: Sri Lanka

Three climates. One short flight. Nine days that do not feel rushed.

Why Sri Lanka

A country the size of a large Indian state, with leopards, tea hills, Dutch ramparts and a coastline, all within a few hours of each other. No internal flights, no long transfers, no jet lag. You land before lunch and are on a beach by evening.

When to go

December to March. The dry season across the south coast, the west coast and the hill country — the lowest rainfall and the most sunshine of the year, and the one window when the beaches, the tea estates and the parks are all good at once.

Seasonal pattern per Intrepid Travel’s Sri Lanka guide

Suggested itinerary

Nine days, one loop, no backtracking. The south coast for the fort and the sea, Yala for the leopards, the hill country by train through the tea, and Sigiriya for the climb. Three landscapes, a car and driver throughout, and nothing booked that you have to rush.

  • Days 1–2Arrive, drive southStraight down the coast road. Nobody should spend a holiday in Colombo.
  • Days 3–4Galle and the south coastThe Dutch fort at dusk, whales off Mirissa, the beaches at Unawatuna.
  • Days 5–6Yala or WilpattuLeopard country. Yala has the density; Wilpattu has the space.
  • Days 7–8The hill countryThe train from Kandy to Ella through the tea estates.
  • Day 9The Cultural TriangleSigiriya at first light, the cave temples at Dambulla on the way back.

Must see

  • Galle Fort at dusk, walked, not driven
  • The Kandy–Ella train through the tea country
  • Sigiriya at opening, before the heat
  • A leopard at Yala, with a tracker who knows the park

Thailand: sixty days became thirty

The rule changed on 15 September. Your plan probably has not.

Thailand's Royal Gazette cut visa-free entry for Indian passports from 60 days to 30, effective 15 September. A further 30 days is possible, but only at the discretion of the immigration officer on the day — never assume it.

How we helpWe check the stay against your dates before you book, not at Suvarnabhumi.

UAE: a British visa alone may not be enough

Turned away at the gate, on a ticket already paid for.

Under updated UAE guidelines, Indian passport holders whose only qualifying document is a UK visa or residence permit are reported to have lost visa-on-arrival. US and EU visa holders are unaffected.

Europe: the transit visa quietly disappeared

One less form, if you are only changing planes.

Spain, France and Germany have each dropped the airport transit visa for Indian nationals connecting on to a non-EU destination — Spain from 4 March, France 10 April, Germany 3 June. It applies to transit, not entry, so the routing matters.

How we helpWe build the connection through a country where the rule applies, so you are not applying for a visa you no longer need.

Been to Europe twice? Stop applying for single entry

One form. Two years of Europe. Same fee as a single trip.

Two Schengen visas used within three years qualifies you for a two-year multi-entry, then usually a five-year. Most people never ask.
